Mission briefing

Introduction

The year is 2149. The invention of the Dark Matter Drive has made traveling between the planets commonplace. Mankind has spread among most of the planets of the solar system.

Unfortunately, the Dark Matter Drive requires energy in the form of diridium, a rare compound that was initially manufactured from asteroids and meteorites that landed on Earth millions of years ago. With the supply of diridium on Earth nearly exhausted, the economic viability of the off-world colonies is threatened.

As a former mining consultant, your economic viability is also threatened by the closure of many Earth mines. Recent advances in mining and construction have made it possible to obtain diridium from large asteroids circling in the asteroid belt. It is dangerous work, but the riskiest jobs usually have the greatest payoff.

The off-world governments have offered financial backing to anyone qualified to construct and manage a two-year mining operation. Six months ago, your application was accepted. Your team of twenty has trained extensively. Today, you risk everything in the hope that your mining operation will bring riches you have only heard and dreamed about.

Field manual

Playing Instructions

Miner 2149 is a simulation based on interconnected formulas. Everything you change can affect the outcome of your colony.

Starting your operation
  • You begin with a 1,000,000 credit stipend to build your colony.
  • Launch up to five probes. Each costs 17,000 credits and reports on one asteroid.
  • Class 1 asteroids are easiest, with more ore and fewer disasters. Class 5 asteroids are the most difficult.
  • Your mother ship supports the first twenty workers with food, quarters, and life support for 21 days.

To begin

  1. Use the building selector at the bottom left to select the bulldozer. The selected tool and its cost appear below the selector.
  2. Tap a smooth area immediately next to the mother ship.
  3. Press +1 on the Advance bar. Bulldozers take one day to clear a space.
  4. Select a building, then tap the clear space to begin construction.
  5. Tap a construction site for project information. Use Undo if you placed the wrong building.
Screen controls and reports

Level — Switch between an asteroid's three levels.

Operations report — Monitor workers, workforce, morale, wage, life support, food, health, occupancy, and deaths.

Production report — Review mines, processor capacity, power, sell price, diridium, storage, and the 30-day projection.

Options — Turn Disaster Mode or gridlines on, save or load games, or resign. Your colony autosaves after every advance.

Advance — Move time forward one, seven, or fourteen days.

Wage — Adjust daily worker pay. A typical wage is 600–700 credits, depending on diridium prices.

Diridium — Tap the storage container to sell processed diridium.

Undo — Reverse the last building placement.

Disaster Mode

Disaster Mode raises the chance of a disaster to roughly ten times normal. Cave-ins, plagues, meteor storms, and power plant explosions stop being setbacks you recover from and become the thing you plan around.

Turn it on from the Options menu. The game asks you to confirm, because it changes the rest of the colony's life:

  • The asteroid class still matters, and it compounds. A Class 1 asteroid in Disaster Mode is dangerous; a Class 5 asteroid in Disaster Mode is close to unsurvivable.
  • Disaster Mode colonies are scored in their own category, separate from normal runs. They are not excluded — a result earned the hard way is still earned.
  • A colony only counts as a Disaster Mode run if Disaster Mode was on for every day of it. Switch it off at any point and the colony is scored as a normal run instead, so you are free to experiment without being locked in.
  • Bringing a colony through the full two years without ever leaving Disaster Mode is the hardest thing in the game, and the only way to earn one particular device frame.

Meteor defense and strategy

During meteor storms, the mobile laser platform moves to the best vantage point. Tap inbound meteors to destroy them. The platform requires power, and firing increases its recharge time.

  • Build only beside a completed building, in the four cardinal directions.
  • Clear smooth terrain before building, but never bulldoze an ore vein.
  • Plan ahead: construction takes time and every structure costs credits.
  • Watch expenses, food, life support, morale, and workforce closely.
  • Build a power plant, space port, mine, life support, and processor early.
