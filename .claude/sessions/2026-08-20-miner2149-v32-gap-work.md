## Decisions Made

All twelve open questions were answered by the user on 2026-08-19 and are recorded in the Master TODO decisions log. The ones that govern future work:

- **Adopt v3.2 as the port target, art included.** Governs O3–O6.
- **Parity rule for mini-games**: taking no action always yields exactly the original outcome; an intervention may only reduce damage. This keeps parity tests valid and the original game fully reachable.
- **The single meteor meter is recharge, not power** — stated in the v3.2 manual and confirmed by `rect(1,120,147,150-f,153,0)`. Full bar means ready to fire. The POWER meter was dropped; power state is communicated by captions, as the original does.
- **Wage guidance is 400–500 credits**, resolved by formula rather than opinion: the morale term is neutral at `sellPrice × (22 + difficulty)`, which is 437–513 across all difficulties at the starting sell price of 19. The site currently prints 600–700 and is wrong.
- **Disaster Mode is unranked**; **auto-resolve ships with the first mini-game**; **versioning starts at `v0.9.0-preview`**; the public roadmap becomes the **Construction Timeline**; the favicon is a **bulldozer silhouette redrawn for 16px**; **colour is deferred entirely**; the `Hi Score:` line is **omitted** from disaster screens (it would collide with the recharge bar).
